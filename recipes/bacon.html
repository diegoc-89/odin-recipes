Cured Bacon

Description

7 day cured pork belly, then smoked with hickory wood for a bold flavor that balances the pork belly's high fat content. Hickory is a strong flavored wood, so using sweet seasonings like brown sugar help balance flavors. Another trick is to combine hickory with mil fruit wood like apple or cherry.

Ingredients

  • Pork Belly
  • 3 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1/3 cup brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp pink curing salt (prague powder #1)
  • Hickory wood and apple or cherry wood if available

Steps

Curing

  1. Combine salts, pepper, sugar, and paprika in a small bowl thoroughly and rub the pork belly with the seasoning getting all over and in any flaps.
  2. Place the pork belly in a 2 gallon zip loc bag in the refrigerator. Every 24 hours take the pork belly out and flip it back into the bag. During the curing process, liquid will begin to be absorbed out of the meat, every day when the pork belly is flipped massage this liquid on the pork belly. This will work like basting and keep your pork belly flavorful.
  3. After 7 days remove the pork belly from the bag, rinse it under cold water, then place on a baking tray with wire rack and pat dry while removing excess seasoning.
  4. Place the pork belly, uncovered on the baking sheet with the wire rack back in the fridge for 24 hours. This will allow the skin to dry and smoke beautifully.

Smoking

  1. Take the pork belly out of the fridge and let it rest while we get our smoker going
  2. Start the smoker, pellet or offset, and once there is a steady temperature between 200-225 degrees fahrenheit, place the pork belly in the smoker and smoke until it reaches and internal temperature of 150f.
  3. Let the bacon rest for 1 hour before wrapping tightly with plastic wrap and let it rest in the refrigerator for 24 hours.
  4. Slice up and enjoy at your leisure!
