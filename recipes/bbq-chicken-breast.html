BBQ Chicken Breast

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp Maggi sauce
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp granulated garlic
  • 1 tsp granulated onion
  • 1 chicken breast
  • 1 tbsp butter

Steps

  1. Combine oil, Worcestershire, and Maggi sauce in a mixing bowl.
  2. With a whisk, mix in the dry ingredients one by one.
  3. Add chicken breast to a quart sized zip loc bag and then add the marinade.
  4. Allow the chicken breast to rest in the marinade at least 2 hours, up to 24 hours.
  5. Heat up a Cast Iron pan on the most on medium high heat for 5 mins, add the butter, then lay the chicken breast in the pan smooth side down.
  6. Cook this first side for 6-8 minutes allowing the flavors ro sear and lock in to the breast. Flip the chicken breast and repeat the last step.
  7. Using a Digital Instant-Read thermometer check the breast, in the thickest part, to be at least 165 degrees for a minimum of 15 seconds before removing from pan.
  8. Let the chicken breast rest for 5 minutes before slicing up and ejoying
