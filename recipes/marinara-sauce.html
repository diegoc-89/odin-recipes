Marinara Sauce

Description

A homemade recipe of marinara sauce that I learned during my years as chef. This recipe uses fresh tomatoes as opposed to canned diced tomatoes like many recipes do.

Ingredients

  • 4lbs ripe tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 large cloves of garlic
  • 1 bunch of fresh basil
  • 1 bunch of fresh oregano
  • 1 bunch of fresh thyme
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • black pepper to taste
  • 1/4 cup of red wine
  • 1 tsp of crushed red pepper

Steps

  1. Cut tomatoes in half, large chop on the onions, and small chop for the garlic.
  2. Heat up a sauce pan on medium high heat for 5 mins.
  3. Place the tomatoes in the pan, cut side down, cover the sauce pot, and let them cook for 6-8 mins.
  4. Take the tomatoes out, take the peel off, and set them to the side.
  5. Add the onions and let them cook for 5 mins before adding the garlic.
  6. Stir and mix frequently and let them cook for another 6 mins.
  7. Once the onions and garlic start to take color, and you can see the bottom of the pot has a good amounf of color to it, add the red wine and deglaze. Turn the heat down to medium and let the wine reduce by half.
  8. Add the tomatoes back in and let that cook for another 7 mins before you start smashing the tomatoes with a wooden spatula or spoon.
  9. Once this has simmered for a good 10 mins start adding the fresh herbs, and seasonings.
  10. Turn the fire off, add to a blender and blend to preferred smoothness.
  11. Taste the sauce and add any seasonings that you feel are needed.
